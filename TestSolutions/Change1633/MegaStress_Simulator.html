MegaStressv3

Re-runs the logic of MEGA_STRESS_TEST.gcb in JavaScript and prints exactly what HSerPrint/HSerSend would send over EUSART1 (RC6, 9600 baud). It is not an instruction-accurate emulator — nothing here executes the compiled .hex on a modelled PIC18 core. A few genuinely hardware-dependent values are estimated and marked ≈ — see the notes below.
